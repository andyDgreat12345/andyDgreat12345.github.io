SLC20 French Club 法语社

Preview

Students will learn beginner level French, including speaking and grammar. They will learn basic French vocabularies, phrases, and verb conjugations. As well, we will present the world of Francophonie, such as French songs.

Club Activities

Our club organizes various activities to make learning French fun and interactive. We have weekly quizzes, group discussions, and cultural events to immerse students in the French culture.
