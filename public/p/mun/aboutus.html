    Welcome to Our Club

    We originate from a debate club with a diverse background, and we focus on Model United Nations (MUN). Our club teaches students about the general procedures of MUN conferences, fostering global awareness essential for becoming better international citizens. We regularly attend open conferences like SISMUN to deepen our understanding of public speaking, global events, and the workings of the United Nations. Additionally, we organize internal conferences for our members.

    What is Model United Nations (MUN)?

    Model United Nations (MUN) is a simulation of the United Nations where participants role-play as delegates representing different countries and attempt to solve real-world issues through diplomacy and negotiation.
