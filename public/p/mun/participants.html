    Welcome to Our Club

    Students in Model United Nations Club will be taught about the general procedures of Model United Nations conferences, as well as global awareness essential to becoming better international citizens. We bring students to open conferences each year, such as SISMUN, to enhance their understanding of public delivery, world events, and United Nations functioning. Moreover, we will host internal conferences for our own students.
